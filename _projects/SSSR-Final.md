**The Goal:** Design and fabricate a soft robotic gripper capable of handling a wide range of objects — from delicate items to heavier, rigid ones — without swapping tooling.

**My Role:** Co-developed a novel hybrid soft robotic gripper within a six-member team, engineering a solution that outperformed either underlying technique alone.

**Project Summary:**
- Combined two complementary soft-robotics techniques into one gripper: kirigami (the Japanese papercraft technique of strategic cuts) for compliant, shape-adapting grasping, and vacuum jamming — friction-locking between loosely packed coffee beans in a vacuum-sealed pouch — for variable, on-demand stiffness.
- Addressed the limitations each technique has on its own: kirigami alone lacks load-bearing capacity, while vacuum jamming alone lacks dexterity for delicate or irregular objects.
- Successfully gripped objects spanning a wide range of size, weight, and fragility — from a paper crane and raspberries to a full soda can and stacked magnetic tiles — validating the hybrid approach.

**Skills Developed:** Soft Robotics, Mechanical Design & Fabrication, Kirigami-based Design, Vacuum Jamming, Rapid Prototyping, Team Collaboration
