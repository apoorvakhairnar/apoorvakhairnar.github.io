**The Goal:** Design a cost-effective, lightweight Static Test Pad (STP) for characterizing the thrust curves and specific impulse of various rocket motors ahead of flight.

**My Role:** Served as Systems Engineer, leading the end-to-end conceptual design of a modular rocket motor test facility, from requirements through 3D CAD delivery and analysis.

**Project Summary:**
- Designed a cost-effective, lightweight static test pad for measuring thrust curves and specific impulse of solid rocket motors prior to flight.
- Developed a single, modular design able to accommodate a range of motor sizes, removing the need for separate test fixtures per motor.
- Produced 3D CAD models and animations in Fusion 360 to communicate and validate the design across the team.

**Skills Developed:** Fusion 360, Conceptual Design, 3D CAD Modeling and Analysis, Team Coordination
