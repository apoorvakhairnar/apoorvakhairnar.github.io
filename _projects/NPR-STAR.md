**The Goal:** Develop a non-pyrotechnic recovery system for high-powered model rockets to improve reliability and reduce cost compared to conventional pyrotechnic systems.

**My Role:** Served as Systems Engineer, leading cross-functional coordination between design and avionics teams to deliver a novel, non-pyrotechnic rocket recovery system.

**Project Summary:**
- Investigated a non-pyrotechnic alternative to conventional recovery systems for high-powered model rockets, aiming to improve reliability and lower cost.
- Kept the recovery mechanism and its triggering electronics developing in sync by aligning the design and avionics workstreams.
- Produced 3D CAD models and animations in Fusion 360 to document and communicate how the recovery system operates.

**Skills Developed:** Fusion 360, Cross-functional Team Leadership, Conceptual Design, 3D Modeling, Analysis & Animation
