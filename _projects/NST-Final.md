**The Goal:** Survey Lyapunov-based nonlinear control techniques for soft robots, focusing on how Sliding Mode Control (SMC) has been implemented and adapted across different soft-robot models.

**My Role:** Authored an independent research survey comparing robust nonlinear control strategies across multiple soft-robot architectures.

**Project Summary:**
- Reviewed how conventional and adaptive SMC have been applied to Piecewise Constant Curvature (PCC) soft arms, and how Cosserat-rod-based SMC better captures the torsion and extension that PCC-based models miss.
- Compared published SMC controllers by actuation type (pneumatic, tendon-driven, twisted-coil), robustness, and whether each was validated experimentally or only in simulation, summarized in a comparison table.
- Identified open challenges — chattering, underactuation, and heavy computation — and outlined promising directions, including model-free approaches like deep learning and reinforcement learning.

**Skills Developed:** Nonlinear Control Theory, Sliding Mode Control, Lyapunov Stability Analysis, Soft Robotics, Literature Review, Technical Writing
