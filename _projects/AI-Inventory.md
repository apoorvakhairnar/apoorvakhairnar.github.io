**The Goal:** Build an automated inventory management system for grocery stores that identifies products by sight — rather than by barcode — enabling real-time stock tracking without added infrastructure.

**My Role:** Co-developed a barcode-free, camera-based inventory recognition system for grocery retail, combining computer vision with a custom tracking interface.

**Project Summary:**
- Trained a CNN-based image recognition model on a labeled dataset of common grocery items, converting camera frames into arrays for classification.
- Built a lightweight GUI that runs on ordinary phone or IP-camera hardware to flag products entering or leaving the shelf in real time, with no specialized equipment required.
- The approach was later featured by Autodesk University as an example of AI's growing role in inventory management, highlighting its potential to reduce stockouts and help predict supply chain disruptions as more data is collected.

**Skills Developed:** Python, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), Computer Vision, Object Detection, SQL Database Design, GUI Development
